steps:
  - "Toss chicken with 1 tbsp cornstarch. Whisk soy, honey, vinegar, the other tbsp cornstarch and water for the sauce."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over high. Cook chicken in one layer until browned and cooked through, 5–6 minutes. Remove."
  - "Add remaining oil, broccoli, pepper and 2 tbsp water. Cover 2 minutes, then uncover and stir-fry 2 minutes."
  - "Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, return chicken, and pour in the sauce."
  - "Stir until glossy and thick, about 1 minute. Serve over rice."
