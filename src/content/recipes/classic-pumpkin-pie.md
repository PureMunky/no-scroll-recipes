steps:
  - "Heat oven to 425°F with a rack in the lower third."
  - "Whisk sugar, salt and spices, then whisk in pumpkin and eggs. Slowly whisk in evaporated milk until smooth."
  - "Pour into the crust and set on a sheet pan."
  - "Bake 15 minutes, then lower to 350°F and bake 40–50 minutes more, until the edges are set and the center has just a slight wobble."
  - "Cool on a rack at least 2 hours to finish setting."
