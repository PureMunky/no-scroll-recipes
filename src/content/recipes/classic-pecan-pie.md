steps:
  - "Heat oven to 350°F with a rack in the lower third."
  - "Whisk eggs, corn syrup, brown sugar, butter, vanilla and salt until smooth. Stir in pecans."
  - "Pour into the crust and set on a sheet pan."
  - "Bake 55–65 minutes, tenting with foil after 30 minutes, until the center is puffed and only slightly wobbly."
  - "Cool at least 2 hours before slicing."
