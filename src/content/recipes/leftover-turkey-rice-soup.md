steps:
  - "Cook onion, carrots and celery in butter over medium until soft, 8 minutes. Add garlic for 30 seconds."
  - "Add broth and thyme and bring to a boil."
  - "Stir in rice and simmer 15–18 minutes, until tender."
  - "Add turkey and salt and simmer 3 minutes to heat through."
  - "Stir in spinach until wilted. Taste for salt."
tip: "Use leftover turkey within 3–4 days, or freeze it in portions for soup later."
