  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "tsp", item: "black pepper" }

steps:
  - "Pour the pan drippings into a measuring cup and let the fat rise. Spoon off the fat, saving up to 4 tbsp to use instead of butter."
  - "Melt the fat or butter in a saucepan over medium. Whisk in flour and cook, whisking, 2 minutes until golden."
  - "Slowly whisk in the warm broth, then the defatted drippings."
  - "Simmer 5–8 minutes, whisking, until it coats a spoon."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and thyme. Strain if you want it extra smooth."
tip: "Make it a day ahead with broth alone, then whisk in the fresh drippings while the turkey rests."
