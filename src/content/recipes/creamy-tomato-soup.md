steps:
  - "Melt butter in a pot over medium. Cook onion until soft, 6 minutes, then garlic for 30 seconds."
  - "Add tomatoes with their juice, broth, sugar and salt. Simmer 15 minutes."
  - "Blend until smooth."
  - "Stir in cream and warm through. Taste for salt."
