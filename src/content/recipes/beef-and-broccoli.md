steps:
  - "Toss beef with 1 tbsp cornstarch. Whisk soy, brown sugar, oyster sauce, sesame oil, water and the other tbsp cornstarch."
  - "Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or skillet over high. Sear beef in batches, 1–2 minutes per side. Remove."
  - "Add remaining oil and broccoli with 2 tbsp water. Cover 2 minutes until bright green."
  - "Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then the beef and sauce."
  - "Toss until the sauce thickens, about 1 minute. Serve with rice."
tip: "Freeze the steak 20 minutes before slicing — it's much easier to cut thin."
