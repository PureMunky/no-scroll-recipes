  - { qty: 6, unit: "", item: "eggs" }

  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "Dijon mustard" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "pickle juice or white vinegar" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { qty: 0, unit: "", item: "paprika, to garnish" }
steps:
  - "Cover eggs with cold water in a pot and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover, and let sit 12 minutes."
  - "Move to ice water for 5 minutes, then peel."
  - "Halve lengthwise and pop the yolks into a bowl."
  - "Mash yolks with mayo, mustard, pickle juice and salt until smooth."
  - "Spoon or pipe back into the whites and dust with paprika."
tip: "Week-old eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones."
