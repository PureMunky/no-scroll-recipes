steps:
  - "Toss chicken with chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, salt, half the lime juice and 1 tbsp oil."
  - "Heat the remaining oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook chicken 5–6 minutes per side until 165°F inside."
  - "Rest 5 minutes, then slice thin and squeeze over the rest of the lime."
  - "Warm tortillas in a dry pan, about 20 seconds per side."
  - "Fill with chicken, cabbage, a spoon of sour cream, cilantro and hot sauce."
tip: "Thighs stay juicy even if you overshoot a minute. Breasts work but pull them at 160°F."
