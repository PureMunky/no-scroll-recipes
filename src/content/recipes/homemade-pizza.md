steps:
  - "Mix flour, yeast, salt and sugar. Add warm water and oil and knead 5–7 minutes until smooth."
  - "Cover and let rise in a warm spot until doubled, about 1 hour."
  - "Heat oven to 500°F with a sheet pan or pizza stone inside."
  - "Divide dough in two and stretch each into a 12-inch round on parchment."
  - "Top with sauce, cheese and toppings. Slide onto the hot pan and bake 10–12 minutes until the crust is browned."
tip: "Make the dough the night before and refrigerate it — it tastes even better."
