steps:
  - "Put carrots, water, butter, honey and salt in a large skillet. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook 6–8 minutes until just tender."
  - "Uncover and boil, stirring, until the liquid reduces to a glossy glaze, 3–5 minutes."
  - "Stir in lemon juice and herbs."
