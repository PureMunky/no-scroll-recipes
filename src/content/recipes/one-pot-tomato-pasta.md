steps:
  - "Put pasta, tomatoes, onion, garlic, pepper flakes, oil, water and salt in a wide pot."
  - "Bring to a boil over high heat, stirring and tossing often with tongs."
  - "Boil 9–10 minutes, stirring, until pasta is al dente and the water has become a glossy sauce."
  - "Off heat, stir in basil and parmesan. Add a splash of water if it tightens up."
