steps:
  - "Pat salmon dry and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat oil in a skillet over medium-high. Cook salmon flesh-side down 4 minutes until golden, then flip."
  - "Lower heat to medium, add butter and garlic, and spoon the foaming butter over the fish for 2–3 minutes until just cooked."
  - "Squeeze in lemon, scatter parsley, and spoon the pan sauce over each fillet."
