---
title: "Stovetop Popcorn"
emoji: "🍿"
tags: ["Snack", "Kid-friendly", "Gluten-free", "Vegetarian"]
time: 10
servings: 4
ingredients:
  - { qty: 3, unit: "tbsp", item: "coconut or neutral oil" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "cup", item: "popcorn kernels" }
  - { qty: 2, unit: "tbsp", item: "butter, melted" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "tsp", item: "fine salt" }
steps:
  - "Heat oil and 3 kernels in a large pot over medium-high, covered."
  - "When those kernels pop, add the rest, cover, and shake the pot."
  - "Shake every few seconds, leaving the lid slightly ajar to let steam out."
  - "When popping slows to 2 seconds between pops, remove from the heat."
  - "Toss with butter and salt."
tip: "Fine salt (or blitzed table salt) sticks far better than coarse."
added: 2026-11-08
---
