  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "whole turkey (12–14 lb), thawed, giblets removed" }
  - { qty: 3, unit: "tbsp", item: "Diamond Crystal kosher salt (or 2 tbsp Morton kosher)" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "black pepper" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "baking powder" }
  - { qty: 4, unit: "tbsp", item: "butter, softened" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "onion, quartered" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "lemon, halved" }
  - { qty: 0, unit: "", item: "a few sprigs fresh thyme, sage or rosemary" }
steps:
  - "1–3 days ahead: pat the turkey very dry inside and out. Mix salt, pepper and baking powder and rub it all over, including under the breast skin."
  - "Set the turkey on a rack over a sheet pan and refrigerate uncovered until roasting day. The skin will look dry and tight — that's what makes it crisp."
  - "Take the turkey out 1 hour before roasting. Heat oven to 425°F with a rack in the lowest position."
  - "Don't rinse. Rub butter over the skin, put the onion, lemon and herbs in the cavity, and tuck the wing tips under."
  - "Roast 30 minutes at 425°F, then lower to 325°F and roast about 2½–3 hours more, until the thickest part of the breast and the inner thigh both read 165°F. Tent the breast with foil if it browns too fast."
  - "Rest at least 30 minutes before carving. Save the pan drippings for gravy."
tip: "Thawing takes longer than people expect: allow 24 hours in the fridge for every 4–5 lb. A 14 lb bird needs about 3 days."
