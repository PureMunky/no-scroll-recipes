  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "", item: "lemon, zested" }
steps:
  - "Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high. Add green beans and 3 tbsp water, then cover."
  - "Steam 4 minutes, then uncover and cook until the water is gone and the beans start to blister, 3–4 minutes."
  - "Add butter and garlic and cook 1 minute."
  - "Season with salt and lemon zest."
