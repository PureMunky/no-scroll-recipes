steps:
  - "Whisk eggs with salt until no streaks remain."
  - "Melt butter in a nonstick pan over medium-low."
  - "Add eggs and stir slowly with a spatula, pushing curds from the edges to the center."
  - "Pull the pan off the heat while the eggs still look slightly wet — they finish cooking on the plate."
  - "Top with chives and pepper."
tip: "Low heat is the whole trick. If the eggs sizzle when they hit the pan, it's too hot."
