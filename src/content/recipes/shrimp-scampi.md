  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "lemon, zested and juiced" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "cup", item: "chopped parsley" }
steps:
  - "Cook linguine in salted water. Save 1 cup pasta water, then drain."
  - "Season shrimp with salt. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high and cook shrimp 1 minute per side. Remove."
  - "Lower heat to medium, add 2 tbsp butter, garlic and pepper flakes for 1 minute."
  - "Add wine and simmer 2 minutes, then the remaining butter, lemon zest and juice."
  - "Toss in pasta, shrimp, parsley and pasta water as needed until silky."
tip: "Pull the shrimp as soon as they turn pink — they finish in the sauce."
