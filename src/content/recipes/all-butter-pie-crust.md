title: "All-Butter Pie Crust"
emoji: "🥧"
tags: ["Dessert", "Baking", "Vegetarian", "Make-ahead"]
time: 75
servings: 8
ingredients:
  - { qty: 1.25, unit: "cup", item: "all-purpose flour" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "sugar" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "cup", item: "cold butter (1 stick), cubed" }
  - { qty: 4, unit: "tbsp", item: "ice water, plus more as needed" }
steps:
  - "Whisk flour, salt and sugar. Cut in the butter with a pastry cutter or your fingertips until the largest pieces are pea-sized."
  - "Drizzle in ice water, tossing with a fork, until the dough holds together when squeezed. Add another tablespoon only if needed."
  - "Press into a disk, wrap, and chill at least 1 hour."
  - "Roll out on a floured surface to a 12-inch circle and ease into a 9-inch pie plate. Fold the overhang under and crimp."
  - "Chill the crust 15 minutes before filling and baking."
tip: "Makes one 9-inch crust. Wrapped well, the dough keeps 3 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen."
